**The model.** With *n* of the 60 seconds observed and summed, the remaining *k* = 60−*n* enter the average diluted by *k*/60, and their sum has standard deviation ≈ σ·k^1.5/√3 — the integrated-variance term, because later seconds have drifted further. **That factor is why certainty accrues so sharply in the last ~20 seconds, and why someone watching a price ticker cannot see it.**

**Burst sampling.** A 20-second sampler sees three of the sixty prints, which makes the entire edge invisible. Inside the final 75s the loop polls the free feeds every ~1.5s. Cheap, keyless, and the difference between *measuring* this edge and merely believing in it.
