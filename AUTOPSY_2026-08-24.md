## The durable lesson

An evidence system must measure the SAME unit the strategy trades. The strategy's unit is the full position lifecycle (entry → tranche lifts → residue); the bucket ledger's unit was "first exit row." When measurement and strategy units diverge, the gate strangles the edge it was built to protect — this is the third accounting-unit bug to cost real money (sold_net winner-selection 8/17, phantom look-ahead 8/20, bucket first-exit 8/24).
